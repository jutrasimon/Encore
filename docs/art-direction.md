# Stickers v1

Created with built-in Imagegen for Encore 0.4.0. Source is preserved unchanged in `dist/art/punk-stickers-v1.png`; `dist/art.js` contains per-object viewBox frames.

Prompt: One transparent 3-column by 6-row atlas of 18 hand-drawn punk die-cut stickers, thick white outlines, saturated lime/orange/cyan/pink inks, scratched black ink hatching and chunky silhouettes. No text, numbers, UI, or tile backgrounds. Row order: patched acoustic guitar, battered microphone, guitar pick; stomping boot, lighter flame, rubber duck; smoke cloud, drink cup, singing earworm; choir, flaming microphone, twisting-neck electric guitar; long musical note, stompbox, repeat arrows; explosive guitar, battered amplifier, jagged sound burst.

Future passes can replace the atlas and its frames without changing game rules.
